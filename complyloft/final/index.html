The Platform

Three things your team probably still does by hand.
We handle all of them.

Whatever documents are piling up: accessible PDFs, redaction requests, regulatory spot-checks — there's a tool in ComplyLoft for it. Your specialists stay in control. They just stop doing the grunt work.

Make Your PDFs Accessible: Without the Pain

The European Accessibility Act is here. If your PDFs aren't accessible to people using screen readers, you're exposed. ComplyLoft auto-tags structure, fixes reading order, and flags what still needs a human eye.

  • ✓ Ready for EAA, WCAG, and PDF/UA requirements
  • ✓ Process one document or ten thousand: same workflow
  • ✓ Handles complex layouts, tables, and multi-column documents
  • ✓ Your team reviews and signs off; nothing goes out unchecked
Learn more about Accessibility →

Stop Redacting Names and Numbers One by One

Every DSAR or FOI request means someone going through hundreds of pages, blacking out personal information. It's slow, repetitive, and error-prone. ComplyLoft detects personal data automatically, cutting that workload by up to 90%.

  • ✓ Automatic detection of names, addresses, IDs, and more
  • ✓ Set your own rules for what gets redacted and how
  • ✓ Built for DSAR and FOI response workflows
  • ✓ Full audit trail; every redaction logged and evidenced
Learn more about Redaction →

Know Exactly Where You Stand Against Any Regulation

Whether you're preparing for a DORA audit, reviewing call centre quality under the Consumer Protection Code, or tracking gaps ahead of an inspection — the Document Auditor checks your documents against any standard you define.

  • ✓ Build scorecards for any regulatory standard, or use ours
  • ✓ Covers DORA, Consumer Protection Code, MiFID II, and more
  • ✓ Clear gap analysis: know exactly what needs fixing
  • ✓ Works for call centre QA, clinical governance, and beyond
