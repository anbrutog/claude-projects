The Problem

Manual Compliance Work Is Holding You Back

Regulated industries face mountains of documents to audit, sensitive data to protect, and accessibility mandates to meet — all handled manually by teams already stretched thin.

Without automation, teams are stuck with:

  • Manually reviewing thousands of documents for compliance gaps
  • PII scattered across documents with no systematic way to redact it
  • PDF libraries that fail accessibility audits under WCAG and EAA
  • Regulatory fines and reputational damage from undetected gaps
  • No audit trail when regulators come knocking

With ComplyLoft, your team gets:

  • AI-powered document auditing across your entire library
  • Automatic PII detection and redaction with a full audit log
  • Bulk PDF remediation to WCAG 2.2 AA and EAA standards
  • Audit-ready evidence packs generated in minutes
  • Complete audit trail for every action taken on every document
