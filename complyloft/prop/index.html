The Starting Point

The compliance market is moving fast. Most websites aren't.

EAA enforcement landed in June 2025. DORA is live. The EU AI Act deadlines are arriving. Compliance buyers are actively searching for solutions right now.

The problem isn't awareness. Most compliance platforms look and sound identical, and their websites do almost nothing to close the gap between a visitor and a conversation.

This is the opportunity ComplyLoft has in front of it.

  • 1Visitors land on a page full of features and walk away with no clear reason to enquire
  • 2Copy is written for search engines, not for the person with a deadline and a regulator breathing down their neck
  • 3There's no funnel — no way to capture interest before someone is ready to buy
  • 4The site carries no urgency, even though the regulatory calendar has built it in for free
  • 5Brand positioning blends into the crowd instead of staking a clear claim

The System

Why this works as a system, not a series of projects

Most businesses commission a website, then SEO, then a funnel — each from a different person with a different brief. They end up inconsistent.

When one person holds the brief across all four workstreams, everything is coherent — from the first Google result to the email that arrives three days after an opt-in.

"The most powerful marketing systems aren't louder. They're more consistent."
