FAQ

Questions answered.

Binaural sound uses slightly different audio frequencies in each ear to create a perceived third tone in the brain — a phenomenon called auditory beat stimulation. When your left ear hears 200Hz and your right ear hears 210Hz, your brain perceives a beat at 10Hz. Depending on the target frequency, this can encourage states associated with calm, focus, or deep sleep. Combined with controlled breathing, the effect is significantly deeper than sound or breathwork alone.
Headphones are strongly recommended — they're what makes the binaural layer work. The effect requires stereo separation between your left and right ears, which speakers can't reliably deliver. Without headphones, you'll still get a well-guided breathing session and the full environmental soundscape, but the binaural frequency layer won't reach its full potential. Any pair of headphones or earphones will work; you don't need anything expensive.
Binaural Breathing isn't a meditation app. There are no guided mindfulness sessions, body scans, or sleep stories. It's a breathing tool built around a specific outcome: shifting your emotional state in minutes. The binaural audio layer and immersive sound environments are purpose-built for breathwork — not adapted from a general wellness platform. Where Calm wants you to relax, Binaural Breathing gives you a physiological tool to actively change how you feel.
